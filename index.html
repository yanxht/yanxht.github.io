Welcome

Welcome to my personal website, a space where I share updates on my professional journey and research endeavors. Currently, I am a Principal Data Science Lead at Microsoft Azure, where I lead the Capacity Intelligence team building AI-powered systems that improve the reliability and capacity efficiency of large-scale cloud infrastructure. Previously I tech-led the Aegis AIOps platform, driving regression detection & prevention and reliability detection for Azure's control plane, and built multi-agent automation frameworks for cloud operations. I earned my PhD in Statistics from Cornell University in 2018, where my research centered on convex optimization for structured feature selection in high-dimensional spaces.
